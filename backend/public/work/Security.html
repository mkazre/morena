Two-factor authentication {{ statusLabel }}

After your password, you’ll also enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy). Someone who steals your password still can’t sign in.
  1. Open your authenticator app and add an account by scanning this code.
  2. Can’t scan? Choose “enter a setup key” and type: {{ secret }}
  3. Type the 6-digit code the app shows to finish.
Save these recovery codes somewhere safe (password manager or printed). Each one signs you in once if you lose your phone. They won’t be shown again.
